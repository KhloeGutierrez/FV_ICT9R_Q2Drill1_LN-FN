Beginner's Guide to Volleyball


📔 Basic Rules

  • Must have 6 players on each team, having a total of 12 players
  • A ball must only be touched 3 times
  • A ball hitting the boundary line is considered in
  • One game consists one 3 or 5 sets
  • Deuce: Each set must be won by 2 points

🏐 Volleyball Equipment

  • A volleyball court
  • A volleyball
  • Players with volleyball attire
  • Protective gear: Kneepads, elbow pads, headbands, wrist bands, etc.

🎯 Basic Techiniques

  • Serving: Underhand, overhead, jump serve, etc.
  • Passing, underhand receiving
  • Settings, overhead receiving
  • Spiking, attacking at the net
  • Blocking, defense at the net
